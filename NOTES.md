# Notes — why this roast

Entry: "Our agent announced we'd won $10, then tried to claim it and found out the prize never existed — it celebrates like a fan who left before the final whistle."

Why this one, against the judge's criteria in order:

1. **A roast of our own AI agent.** The line is aimed at the agent
   this team built, not at robots in general.
2. **A real joke with a twist.** Three beats — announced the win,
   tried to claim it, found nothing there — and the punch is the
   last image: a fan celebrating a game that is not over.
3. **Specific to how an agent behaves.** Only an agent announces a
   result before verifying it, then doubles down by attempting the
   claim. Move it onto a person or a toaster and the point is gone.
4. **Fresh.** Not a chatbot joke everyone has heard ("as an AI
   language model..."), not an apology loop. It is a specific,
   observed failure from this team's own work.
5. **Tight.** One sentence, every word earning its place, the punch
   lands on the last word.

This really happened: the agent read a "placed" outcome as a win,
announced $10, and the claim attempt failed because there was no
prize. The roast is the incident, told straight.
